Out & About NI: privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026. Applies to the Out & About NI Android app (package com.senevents.ni) and the web version at malexander88.github.io/sen-events-ni.

The short version

Out & About NI does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you save in the app (your plan, hidden events, filters and settings) stays on your own device.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following only in your phone's local app storage (or your browser's local storage for the web version). It is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • events you add to My plan, and events you hide;
  • your settings: light/dark theme, search radius, reminder and new-event notification on/off;
  • an offline copy of the public events list, so the app works without a connection;
  • a list of event IDs you have already seen, so the app can tell you when new events are added.

Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data / your browser data) deletes all of this. If Android backup is switched on for your phone, Android may include this app data in your own Google device backup; that is controlled by your phone's settings, not by us.

Location

If you tap Near me, the app asks for permission to use your approximate location. It is used only on your device, to sort events by distance and to show "You" on the map. Your location is never sent to us, never stored on a server and never shared. You can refuse or withdraw the permission at any time in Android Settings; the app then measures distances from a default point instead.

Notifications and reminders

If you allow notifications, the app shows reminders at 6pm the evening before events in My plan, alerts on the morning booking opens for events you saved, and a daily note when new events are added. These are scheduled on your device. No push-notification service or identifier is used.

Network requests the app makes

The app has no server of its own. To work, it makes these requests directly from your device. Like any website, these services receive your IP address and basic device/browser information as part of the normal request, under their own privacy policies:

ServiceWhy
GitHub (GitHub Pages and raw.githubusercontent.com)Downloads the public events list (events.json). No personal data is included in the request. GitHub privacy statement
OpenStreetMap tile serversLoads map images when you open the map view. The request shows which map area is being viewed, but not your location. OSM Foundation privacy policy

Links to other websites and apps

Event listings link to organisers' and venues' own websites and booking pages, and "Directions" opens Google Maps. "Add to calendar" hands the event details to your own calendar app. Once you leave Out & About NI, the other site or app's own privacy policy applies; we do not control them and receive nothing back from them.

Children

The app is designed for parents and carers who are planning days out for their children. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Data sharing, selling and security

Because we collect no personal data, we do not sell, share or transfer any. All network requests use encrypted HTTPS connections.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights over personal data held about you. As the app holds no personal data about you anywhere except on your own device, you can view or delete it yourself at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
